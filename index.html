About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at Cornell University.

My research is in Econometrics. I am interested in evidence aggregation and robustness analysis, and in my work I use tools from causal inference, partial identification and statistical decision theory to help researchers achieve more credible answers to policy-relevant questions. I am on the 2026-2027 job market.

I hold a M.Sc. in Mathematical Modelling and a B.Sc. in Economics from Fundação Getulio Vargas (FGV) - Rio de Janeiro.
